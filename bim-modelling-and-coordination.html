BIM modelling and coordination

A steel model the other trades can coordinate against, in the formats they ask for.

On coordinated projects the steel model is used by everyone, not only the shop. We keep it current through design revisions and export it for the coordination meetings.

What you get

  • IFC exports of the steel model at agreed milestones
  • Clash checks against the architectural and services models you provide
  • Model updates as design revisions are issued
  • 3D views and viewer files for shop and erection planning

What we need from you

  • The project’s BIM execution plan, if there is one
  • Models from the other trades in IFC or native format
  • The coordination meeting schedule

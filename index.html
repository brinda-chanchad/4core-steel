How a job moves through our office

Scope and schedule

We review the contract drawings, list what is in and out of scope, and agree issue dates before modelling starts.

Model and query

The structure is modelled in 3D. Anything unclear in the design drawings goes to you as a numbered RFI, not a guess.

Check and issue

A second detailer checks every sheet against the model and the contract set. Then drawings go out for approval.

Release to the shop

Approval comments are picked up, revisions are clouded, and the final set goes out with material lists and CNC files.

Two pens on every sheet

A detailing error is cheapest to fix on paper and most expensive to fix on site. So no drawing leaves us on one person’s word. A checker who did not draw the sheet goes over it with two colours.

  • Yellow marks each dimension, piece mark and quantity the checker has verified against the model and the contract drawings.
  • Red marks a correction or an open query. A sheet with red on it is not issued.
  • Revisions made after approval are clouded and numbered, so the shop sees exactly what changed.

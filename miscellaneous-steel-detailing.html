Miscellaneous steel detailing

Stairs, handrails, ladders, platforms and the other small steel that takes the most drawing time.

Miscellaneous steel is where architectural drawings and site conditions matter most. We detail it from the architectural set as well as the structural one, and raise a query wherever the two disagree.

What you get

  • Stair and landing drawings with stringers, treads and connections
  • Guard rail and handrail drawings, checked against the dimensions on the contract set
  • Ladder, cage and platform drawings
  • Loose lintels, frames, bollards and embeds
  • Field measure sheets where existing conditions govern

What we need from you

  • Architectural plans, sections and stair details
  • Finish requirements: primed, galvanized or painted
  • Site measurements for any work that meets existing structure

Other services

  • Structural steel detailing
  • Connection detailing support
  • Estimation and material take-off
  • BIM modelling and coordination
  • CNC and fabrication data

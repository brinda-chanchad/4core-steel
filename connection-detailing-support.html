Connection detailing support

Connections modelled to the engineer’s design and checked for fit-up before bulk drawings start.

We detail connections from the design drawings and your connection engineer’s calculations. Where the contract calls for delegated connection design, we coordinate with the licensed engineer you appoint and model exactly what is stamped.

What you get

  • Shear, moment and bracing connections modelled to the design
  • Checks on bolt access, weld access and erection clearance
  • Connection sketches for approval before bulk drawings start
  • A numbered RFI log for every open connection query

What we need from you

  • Connection schedules or stamped calculations
  • Your shop’s preferred bolts, hole types and weld processes
  • The name of the connection engineer, if design is delegated

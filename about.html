About

A steel detailing office built around one habit: nobody checks their own drawings.

Who we are

4CoreSteel Engineering Services is a structural steel detailing firm. We work as the drawing office for steel fabricators and erectors: you send the contract set, and we return the shop drawings, erection drawings and fabrication data your shop needs.

We keep the team small enough that the people who scope your job are the people who model it.

Four things we hold to

Nobody checks their own work

Every sheet is checked by a detailer who did not draw it, senior staff included.

Queries go out early

A gap in the design drawings becomes a numbered RFI the day we find it, not a note on the approval set.

Dates are agreed up front

You get an issue schedule before modelling starts, and a status update against it every week.

One person answers for the job

Each project has a single lead who knows the model and replies to your emails.

How we work with your team

Drawings are produced on your title block, with your numbering and your shop standards. Each week you receive a status sheet: drawings issued, drawings in checking, and every open RFI with the date it was raised.

If the design changes mid-job, we tell you what it affects before we change anything, so you can decide how to handle it with your customer.

Have contract drawings ready?

Send the structural and architectural set. We reply with a scope, a sheet estimate and a schedule.

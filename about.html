About

A steel detailing office built around one habit: nobody checks their own drawings.

Who we are

4CoreSteel Engineering Services is a structural steel detailing firm. At 4Core Steel Engineering Services, quality is not the final step — it is part of every step. We believe accurate steel detailing comes from technical expertise, disciplined workflows, and independent checking.

Our team provides reliable steel detailing solutions to steel fabricators, helping transform structural designs into clear, accurate, and fabrication-ready drawings. Every project passes through a structured review process, where independent checking helps identify errors, reduce rework, and maintain consistency before drawings are issued.

With a dedicated team of professionals and experience using SDS2, we focus on delivering detailed shop drawings, erection drawings, and connection-related detailing support tailored to project requirements.

We understand that every drawing matters — because on-site efficiency, fabrication accuracy, and project schedules depend on the quality of the details behind them.

At 4Core, we don’t just detail steel. We build confidence into every connection, every drawing, and every project.

Our Principle: The person who creates the drawing should not be the only person who checks it.

How we work with your team

Reliable Detailing. Seamless Coordination. Quality You Can Trust.

At 4Core Steel Engineering Services, we provide reliable steel detailing support tailored to your project requirements, standards, and deadlines.

  1. 01

    Understand Your Requirements

    We review your project scope, structural drawings, specifications, connection requirements, and detailing standards before starting work.

  2. 02

    Coordinate With Your Team

    We follow your preferred workflow and maintain clear communication with your project managers, engineers, and detailers throughout the project.

  3. 03

    Detail With SDS2

    Using SDS2, we develop accurate 3D steel models, shop drawings, erection drawings, and CNC data as required by the project scope.

  4. 04

    Independent Quality Checks

    Our structured checking process ensures that drawings are reviewed by someone other than the original detailer, helping identify errors and maintain quality before submission.

  5. 05

    Submit and Revise

    We submit drawings according to agreed schedules, address your comments, and incorporate revisions in coordination with your team.

  6. 06

    Deliver With Confidence

    We aim to support your fabrication schedule through consistent communication, dependable turnaround times, and detailing that follows your project requirements.

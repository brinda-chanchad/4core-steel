CNC and fabrication data

NC files, DXF files and reports taken straight from the checked model.

Files for your machines come from the same model as the drawings, after checking, so the part the beam line cuts is the part on the sheet.

What you get

  • DSTV NC files for beam lines and drill lines
  • DXF files for plate cutting
  • Exports for your production or MRP system on request
  • Cut lists, bolt lists and shipping lists

What we need from you

  • The machines and software your shop runs
  • Your part and assembly numbering rules
  • Any file naming convention your system requires
